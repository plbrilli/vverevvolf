A party game, hosted by a vampire

One of you is a wolf. I know which. I might tell you.

Vverevvolf is the old village-and-werewolves game with one change: nobody has to sit out and run it. The Count does. He narrates the night, announces the dead, and remembers every lie you told at the table.

How a night goes

I.

Phones out, eyes open

Everyone gets a secret role on their own phone. Wolves choose a victim, the Seer peeks, the Doctor guards, all with a silent tap. No one sits out.

II.

The Count speaks

Over one speaker, he tells you who didn't survive and how. He uses your names, recalls last round's accusations, and is never once sympathetic.

III.

The village decides

Argue, accuse, vote, execute. The Count keeps the ledger. Then night falls again, until the wolves are dead or the village is.

Your host

“I have watched villages and wolves tear each other apart for four hundred years. You will not surprise me. But you may amuse me.”

The Count is an AI narrator with a character, not a rules engine with a voice. The game's code holds the secrets; he only performs them. So he can tease, flatter, and mislead without ever breaking the game.
